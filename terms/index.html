What the app is

Speakeasy gives you two words and a short timer, and asks you to talk. It then shows you what you said and some plain measurements of how you said it: your pace, your pauses, and words you leaned on.

It is practice, not instruction. Speakeasy does not grade you, does not certify anything, and makes no promise about how your speaking will change. Nothing in the app is professional, medical, educational or therapeutic advice.
