Privacy Policy

Speakeasy · last updated 24 August 2026

Short version: your voice never leaves your phone, because it is never recorded. Everything else is off by default: signing in is optional, and so is the one feature that sends the text of a talk anywhere. Both are described below, and both can be switched off.

Your voice

Speakeasy uses the microphone only while a drill is running. Your speech is turned into text by your own device, using Apple's on-device speech recognition. The app requires on-device transcription and will refuse to run rather than send your audio anywhere. The audio itself is never written to disk and is discarded the moment the drill ends. There is no recording to play back, because none is kept. This is true whether or not you sign in.

What is stored on your device

Your talks, meaning the two words, the transcript, the pace, the pauses and the date, are saved in a single file on your own device. They are not uploaded and they are not shared. They are included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup if you have that switched on, the same as any other app's data.

Deleting the app deletes them. Settings also has a "Delete all history" button that removes everything without uninstalling, and you can delete a single talk by long-pressing it in History.

The optional account

Speakeasy works completely without an account, and it does not ask for one to unlock anything. An account exists for one reason: so your streak survives changing phones.

If you choose to sign in, Speakeasy uses Sign in with Apple. The app requests no personal information from Apple: it does not ask for your name and it does not ask for your email address, so it never receives either one. Apple returns an anonymous identifier for your account and nothing else.

What is then stored on the server, which is hosted by Supabase:

  • that anonymous identifier
  • a username you type in yourself, which does not have to be your real name
  • the number of days you have practiced, and the date of the most recent one

What is never sent to the server, signed in or not: your audio, your transcripts, your results, the words you were given, and your email address.

Deleting your account

Settings has a "Delete account" button. It erases your record on the server, including the username and the practice count. Your talks are stored separately on your own device and are not touched by that button; delete those with "Delete all history" or by deleting the app.

You can also stop using the account at any time by signing out, which leaves the app fully working.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled by your own device. No push token is registered, nothing is sent to a server, and turning it off in Settings removes every pending reminder.

The home screen widget

The widget reads today's two words, which are computed on the device from the date, and your streak count, which is stored in your device's keychain so that the widget can see it. Neither leaves the phone.

The connection check, if you switch it on

Speakeasy can check whether you actually linked the two words you were given, rather than just saying both. That is a judgment about meaning, so it is the one thing in the app that cannot be worked out on your phone by counting, and the one feature that sends any part of a talk anywhere.

It is off until you turn it on, in Settings, behind a screen that says all of this before you agree. With it on:

  • the text of your talk, and the two words, are sent to Google's Gemini API, through a server of ours that holds the key so the key never ships inside the app. It is sent once while you are still speaking, so the answer is ready the moment you stop, and again at the end only if what you said moved on a long way
  • Google's own terms and privacy policy govern what they do with that text
  • your audio is never sent, because it is never recorded
  • your name, your email, your streak, your history and every other talk are never sent
  • turning the switch off stops it immediately, and the rest of the results screen is unaffected

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party tracking SDKs, no crash reporting services, no cookies. Nothing about how you use the app is collected, profiled or transmitted, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone for advertising or any other purpose. The app makes three kinds of network request. Two are yours to choose: signing in, and switching on the connection check. The third is not a choice, and it carries nothing about you: the phone downloads Apple’s speech model once, over an unmetered connection, and that download is what allows the transcription described above to run on your own device rather than on somebody’s server.

Withdrawing permission

You can revoke the app's access to the microphone or to speech recognition at any time in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, or by finding Speakeasy in the list of apps. The app will tell you it cannot run and will not attempt to work around it. Nothing you have already said is affected, because no audio is kept.

How long things are kept

Audio: not at all. It exists only in memory for the length of a drill. Transcripts and statistics: on your device until you delete them. Account record: on the server until you delete the account. There is no expiry and no separate archive.

Children

Speakeasy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If an account was created by a child under 13, write to the address below and it will be deleted.

Purchases

Speakeasy is currently free and offers nothing to buy. If that ever changes, purchases would be handled entirely by Apple, payment details would never be seen by the app, and this policy would be updated first.
