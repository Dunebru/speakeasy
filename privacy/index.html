Privacy Policy

Speakeasy · last updated 18 August 2026

Short version: nothing you do in Speakeasy leaves your phone. There is no account, no analytics, and no server. The app makes no network requests at all.

No account

There is no sign-up and no sign-in. The app has no way of knowing who you are, and it never asks.

Your voice

Speakeasy uses the microphone only while a drill is running. Your speech is turned into text by your own device, using Apple's on-device speech recognition — the app requires on-device transcription and will refuse to run rather than send your audio anywhere. The audio itself is never written to disk and is discarded the moment the drill ends. There is no recording to play back, because none is kept.

What is stored, and where

Your talks — the two words, the transcript, the pace, the pauses and the date — are saved in a single file on your own device. They are not uploaded, shared, or backed up anywhere by the app. They are included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup if you have that switched on, the same as any other app's data.

Deleting the app deletes them. Settings also has a “Delete all history” button that removes everything without uninstalling.

Withdrawing permission

You can revoke the app's access to the microphone or to speech recognition at any time in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, or by opening Settings and finding Speakeasy in the list of apps. The app will tell you it cannot run and will not attempt to work around it. Nothing you have already recorded is affected, because nothing is kept.

How long things are kept

Audio: not at all. It exists only in memory for the length of a drill and is discarded when the drill ends. Transcripts and statistics: until you delete them. There is no expiry, no server copy, and no way for anyone else to reach them. Delete them individually by long-pressing a talk in History, all at once from Settings, or by deleting the app.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs, no crash reporting services, no cookies. Nothing about your use of the app is collected or transmitted.

Purchases

Speakeasy Pro is an auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether a subscription is currently active. Payment details are never seen by the app or by us. Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.
